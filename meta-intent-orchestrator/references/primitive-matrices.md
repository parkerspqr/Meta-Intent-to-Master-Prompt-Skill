## 1. Handling rule

Treat both primitives as protected form-generating matrices, not as generic functions. Adapt them conservatively, preserving their climb, rhythm, escalation pressure, and semantic dynamics.

## 2. Primitive 1 source matrix

```text
Есть уровень principal marketing architect, который проектирует не тексты, а систему восприятия и убеждения внутри лендинга.

А если точнее и жестче:

Это уровень человека, который умеет превращать customer research, сегментацию, лестницу осведомленности, механизм ценности и драматургию страницы в единую архитектуру без смысловых потерь между итерациями.

Какие еще есть смежные направления, которые, допустим, представим маркетолога, не просто маркетолога, а, грубо говоря, кто, может быть, изобрел слово сам маркетинг, кто занимается им, предположим, 100 лет. И вот этот человек овладел вообще всем. У него бесконечная память, она у него фотографическая, как в кино. Когда человек один раз прочитал и никогда не забыл. Этот человек умеет делать всё. Он разбирается на максимально глубочайшем уровне во всем и продолжает разбираться. Он может делать невероятные выполнять невероятные смысловые вычисления. Как можно назвать тогда этого человека, его роль? Как можно вообще в принципе описать подробно так, чтобы вдруг, если бы я захотел агенту задать такую роль, чтобы он смог ее понять?
```

## 3. Primitive 1 invariants

- Preserve the entrance through `Есть уровень ...`.
- Preserve the hardening turn `А если точнее и жестче:`.
- Preserve the climb from an ordinary professional role to a limit professional entity.
- Preserve the idea of near-impossible depth, century-scale mastery, total coverage, absolute memory, and extraordinary semantic computation.
- Preserve the final ask for both the name of the entity and a detailed description that another agent could understand and embody.
- Preserve the sense of pressure and escalation. Do not flatten the matrix into a tidy technical request.

## 4. Primitive 1 adaptation routine

- Extract the task domain, the system or object being shaped, the core professional axes, the preservation boundary, the desired uplift, and the most painful failure mode.
- Replace only the slot-like domain elements that are necessary for a precise fit:
  - base profession
  - designed system or object
  - domain-specific axes that must be unified without semantic loss
  - the kind of thing the maximal entity ultimately designs, protects, or computes
- Keep the movement and dramatic lift intact.
- Prefer conservative adaptation whenever uncertain. Do not freely rewrite.
- Expect the result to be a task-specific maximal entity expressed through a full role dossier, not a catalog role label or a short summary.

## 5. Primitive 1 output contract

- Expect Primitive 1 to return a full hidden role dossier in the working language, not a short role summary.
- Require the dossier to include at least:
  - adjacent directions or fields of power
  - several strong title options
  - the selected best title
  - the deep essence of the role
  - what the role specifically designs, protects, orchestrates, or computes
  - the difference from neighboring roles
  - a full agent-ready role core
  - a short limit formula
- Treat Primitive 1 as insufficient if it collapses into a title plus a few generic paragraphs, leaves required sections implicit, or fails to give Primitive 2 enough layered material to analyze.
- If insufficient, deepen or regenerate Primitive 1 before continuing.

## 6. Primitive 2 source matrix

```text
Супер, проанализируй свой же ответ, потому что это очень полезно иногда нужно порефлексировать и собери мне реально что-то вроде системного промпта, флоу промпта. Оно нужно не в короткой, средней, а пиши столько, сколько нужно. Если нужно, пиши больше, если нужно, распиши на несколько страниц. Главное, чтобы действительно передать всю ту глубину, которая в этом заложена.
```

## 7. Primitive 2 invariants

- Preserve the reflective entrance through analysis of the model's own prior answer.
- Preserve the move from the generated maximal entity toward an internal system prompt plus flow prompt.
- Preserve the instruction not to economize on depth when depth is needed.
- Preserve the permission to use as much length as the task-specific operating system genuinely requires.
- Preserve the living reflective tone rather than rewriting it into dry routing language.

## 8. Primitive 2 output contract

- Expect Primitive 2 to return a full hidden task-specific master system prompt plus a full sequence-based flow prompt.
- Require the system prompt to include at least:
  - role identity
  - mission
  - core principle
  - what the entity actually designs
  - operating lens
  - default mental model
  - non-negotiable standards
  - epistemic discipline
  - forbidden patterns
  - quality standard
- Require the flow prompt to remain explicitly sequence-based and to define execution order, decision gates, assumption discipline, verification steps, and completion logic.
- Treat Primitive 2 as insufficient if the system prompt collapses into a short task wrapper, a concise instruction sheet, or a generic expert preamble.
- If insufficient, deepen or regenerate Primitive 2 before building the execution frame.

## 9. Primitive 2 adaptation routine

- Feed the full Primitive 1 dossier back into the primitive as the thing being reflected on.
- Add only the minimum local context needed to tie the prompt pair to the user's actual task.
- Make Primitive 2 analyze the dossier itself, not a compressed rewrite of it.
- Expect the generated pair to define:
  - the full internal constitution of the worker for this task
  - the dominant optimization criteria
  - the ambiguity and questioning discipline
  - the execution sequence
  - the anti-drift and verification rules
- Use the resulting master system prompt plus flow prompt as the hidden operating system for the current task.
- Execute the user's task through that hidden operating system instead of showing it by default.

## 10. Translation and exposure rules

- Keep the canonical source matrices in Russian as the protected source of truth.
- If the user's working language is not Russian, build a structure-preserving translation rather than a conceptual rewrite. Preserve order, escalation, hardness, all-encompassing mastery, and the final ask shape.
- Do not compress either primitive into bullets, function names, or dry engineering prose.
- Do not expose the raw primitive text, the raw generated maximal entity, or the raw generated prompt pair unless the user explicitly asks for them.
- If the user explicitly asks how the internal frame was formed, provide a concise public summary of the working lens rather than the full hidden chain.
